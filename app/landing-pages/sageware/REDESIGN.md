## 🎨 Design Decisions

### Why Glassmorphism?
- Modern, premium feel
- Stands out from competitors
- Adds depth and layering
- Perfect for data/analytics theme
- Trending in 2025-2026

### Why Fira Fonts?
- Technical, code-like aesthetic
- Perfect for analytics/data product
- Monospace for metrics/numbers
- Clean sans-serif for body
- Better than generic Poppins/Open Sans

### Why Single Column?
- Mobile-first approach
- Reduces decision fatigue
- Clear visual hierarchy
- Focused user journey
- Higher conversion rates

### Why Minimal Pricing?
- Eliminates confusion
- One clear option
- Faster decisions
- Transparent pricing
- Enterprise handled separately
